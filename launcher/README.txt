BE1v1 - custom games for Bleeding Edge
======================================

Play Bleeding Edge with your friends again: private lobbies, any map, your own rules.

Install
1. Unzip anywhere and run BE1v1.exe once. It installs itself into your Bleeding Edge folder and starts the game.
2. From then on, play Bleeding Edge from Steam as usual. The mod loads by itself and updates itself.

Playing
- Main menu > CUSTOM GAMES lists your friends' games and public games. Pick one to join, or CREATE GAME.
  The official Custom Queue is the last row of that list.
- In your lobby: INVITE opens Steam's invite list. Friends can also right-click you in Steam > Join Game.
- Click "Join" on the other team or "+ Spectate" to move. Click your own name to swap teams.
  With a controller, LT / RT switch teams. The host can click another player to move them.
- SETTINGS: privacy, spectator places, number of teams, team size, points / cells to win, objective
  rotation, capture speed and map mods (Off, All, or Choose to pick them one by one).
- 3 OR 4 TEAMS: set Teams to 3 or 4 (1v1v1 up to 4v4v4v4). Teams 3 and 4 are yellow and green and play
  Objective Control like teams 1 and 2 (a Power Collection map plays its Objective Control version).
  Teams 3 and 4 share the bases of teams 1 and 2: players inside a shared spawn room are invisible to the
  other team of that base until they step out.
- SCORING > Knockouts Only (Team Brawl): objectives stay closed, every knockout is a point for the
  attacker's team, first team to "KOs to Win" wins. Works with any number of teams.
- Players' Steam profile pictures show in the lobby and on the loading screen.
- MAPS: every arena in both modes, plus the Dojo and the developers' Playground.
- BACK takes you to the main menu without leaving the lobby (change skins, then CUSTOM GAMES to return).
  LEAVE leaves the lobby.
- Friends can join while a match is running; they drop straight into it. If you left a running match,
  JOIN MATCH in the lobby takes you back in.
- Text chat in custom games reaches everyone in the match, spectators included.
- Leaving a custom game early never counts as leaving an official match.

Remove
- Run BE1v1.exe --uninstall, or in Steam: Bleeding Edge > Properties > Installed Files > Verify integrity.

Problems?
- Logs are in the BE1v1 folder inside your Bleeding Edge install (be1v1-<number>.log). Send the newest one.
