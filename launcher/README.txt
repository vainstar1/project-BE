BE1v1 - custom games for Bleeding Edge
======================================

Play Bleeding Edge with your friends again: private lobbies, any map, your own rules.

Install
1. Unzip anywhere and run BE1v1.exe once. It installs itself into your Bleeding Edge folder and starts the game.
2. From then on, just play Bleeding Edge from Steam as usual. The mod loads by itself and keeps itself up to date.

Play
- Main menu > CUSTOM GAME creates a lobby. INVITE opens Steam's invite list.
- Friends can also join you from the Steam friends list (right-click > Join Game).
- Pick maps and settings, choose teams, press START.

Remove
- Run BE1v1.exe --uninstall, or use Steam > Bleeding Edge > Properties > Installed Files > Verify integrity.

Problems?
- Logs are in the BE1v1 folder inside your Bleeding Edge install (be1v1-<number>.log).
